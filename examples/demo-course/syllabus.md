# CELL101 Foundations of Cell Biology

## Learning objectives

- Explain how phospholipid structure produces a selectively permeable membrane.
- Compare passive transport, facilitated diffusion, and active transport.
- Predict the direction of water movement under different tonicities.

## Assessment integrity

The practice questions are formative. For assessed reports, students may ask for concept explanations and feedback, but submitted analysis and wording must remain their own.
