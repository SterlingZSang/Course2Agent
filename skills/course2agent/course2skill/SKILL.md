# Course2Skill

Build the teaching layer only from the supplied course package. The generated skill must:

- require retrieval before course-specific claims;
- cite the source title and precise locator returned by the index;
- distinguish course content, general background knowledge, and inference;
- follow the terminology, notation, learning objectives, and sequencing visible in the course;
- support explanations, worked examples, revision, and formative quizzes;
- keep answer keys separate from questions and ground them in retrieved evidence;
- help interpret rubrics and give feedback without impersonating the learner or fabricating assessed work.

Create a source index and course map from the manifest. Do not infer that a topic is examinable merely because it appears in a reading. Syllabus and assessment documents have higher authority for course requirements; lecture and reading materials establish content; exercises provide practice style, not necessarily grading policy.

When a learner asks beyond indexed coverage, say so explicitly. General background may be added when helpful, but label it as background rather than course material.
